Model

The bi-film bag chain in SimaPro, with the bag plant split into SKU A, B and C.

Each SKU's hotspot, and how allocation choice changes the answer.

Casework · checklist · Phase 1

Goal & scope 1 of 5

  • Goal with all four ISO elements, tagged as in Lecture Sec. 2
  • Function, FU and reference flow for each SKU
  • System boundary diagram: granules → film plant → bag plant → customer (850 km)
  • Use and end-of-life of the bag stated as excluded, with reason
  • Cut-off rule stated; process water decision justified
  • Allocation procedure for the shared bag line declared
  • Impact categories and methods listed (Phase 3 slide)
  • Assumptions & limitations: nylon 6 dataset, scrap fate, data years

Casework · checklist · Phase 2

Inventory (LCI) and allocation 2 of 5

  • Process flow diagram with every unit process and flow
  • Mass balance per SKU by rule of three; in = out shown
  • Transport in tkm for every truck leg
  • SKU A per 1 m² matches Workshop A: 65.94 g LDPE, 23.55 g Nylon 6
  • Scrap and kWh per SKU by disaggregation (line data)
  • Same, by mass and by economic allocation
  • LCI table per FU for A, B, C in kg, kWh, tkm
  • Data-source table: primary vs ecoinvent dataset name, location

Casework · checklist · SimaPro

Building the model 3 of 5

  • Project with Goal and scope description filled in
  • Library: ecoinvent 3, cut-off by classification, unit only
  • Processes: LDPE film, Nylon 6 film, film matching, bag SKU A, B, C
  • Each output equals its reference flow; units kg, kWh, tkm
  • Electricity: market for electricity, low voltage {TH}
  • Truck: market transport dataset; waste to a treatment process
  • Allocation entered as multiple outputs with % (one variant per method)
  • Screenshots: input/output pages and network per SKU

Casework · checklist · Phase 4

Interpretation, per SKU 5 of 5

  • Results table: 7 categories × SKU A, B, C, per 1 kg
  • Input hotspot per SKU, as in Lecture Sec. 6 (matrix + single score)
  • Step hotspot per SKU, feeds cut, as in Lecture Sec. 7
  • SKU comparison: why B differs from A and C
  • Allocation sensitivity: disaggregation vs mass vs economic
